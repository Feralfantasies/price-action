## 2026-09-17 (PR #8 review round 1)
* **Update**: Corrected the replay classification of `quantity` — it is no longer listed as cosmetic: [Configuration](configuration.md) now states that it sizes every notional (committed funds, per-leg fees, cash/equity movements and trade P/L) and determines entry affordability; `README.md` groups it with the other paper-accounting settings. [Paper Trading Accounting](paper-trading-accounting.md) gained a table row for it.
* **Update**: Defined an explicit short-position insolvency policy in `src/accounting.rs` (previously only "never negative" was guaranteed, unimplemented): a closing loss beyond what closing frees floors free funds at 0 — the shortfall is a write-off in that bar's mark, never reported as negative debt; equity likewise floors at 0. Closed-trade rows keep the full arithmetic P/L regardless. Documented in [Paper Trading Accounting](paper-trading-accounting.md) (new insolvency bullet), with margining/stop-loss explicitly out of scope; `mark_equity` and `exit_position` doc updated.
* **Update**: Roll-ups cover open positions at session end: the per-UTC-day table counts an entry on that day, and `SessionTotals.total_fees_paid` now includes the still-open entry leg (via booked-so-far fees). Covered by new unit tests; verified live with threshold=1 runs.
* **Update**: [Replay Workflow](replay-workflow.md) "Read-only, always" guarantee reworded: replay never sends orders to any broker, but it *does* simulate accounting costs (committed funds/collateral, per-side fees, cash/equity) for reporting — all in memory.
